### 4.3 Venue halts withdrawals for 72 h (and venue failure)

If deposits to the venue also stop, no top-up is possible; a sleeve is liquidated when the price rises by more than: SPY 31.8%, NVDA 29.4%, AAPL 29.4% within the halt.
