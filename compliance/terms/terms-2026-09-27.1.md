## 1. What Stockline is

Stockline is software that lets you interact with smart contracts on Robinhood Chain: lend Stock Tokens into Morpho
Vault V2 vaults (`rSTOCK`) and borrow Stock Tokens against USDG collateral in Morpho Blue markets. Stockline does not
hold your assets, does not give advice and cannot reverse transactions.

## 2. Who may use it

You may not use Stockline if you are located in, resident in, or a citizen of a restricted jurisdiction: the United
States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United Arab Emirates, or any comprehensively sanctioned
jurisdiction or region, or if you are on a sanctions list. Opening a borrow requires a compliance attestation
(IP-country check, sanctions screen and acceptance of these terms). Repaying, closing, withdrawing and unwrapping
never require one, so you can always leave.

## 3. Risks you accept

- **Liquidation.** If your health factor falls below 1.0 your collateral can be liquidated, at a loss to you
  (liquidation incentive ~7.4% at 77% LLTV). Prices can move against a short without limit.
- **Weekends, holidays and earnings.** While the price feed is closed, the oracle marks borrowed stock up by a safety
  buffer (ramping in before the close and before scheduled earnings). Your health factor falls during the ramp even if
  the market does not move. Prices can gap when the market reopens.
- **Variable rates.** Borrow rates and lender yields are variable and can rise sharply when a pool is fully used.
  Displayed APYs are variable and not a promise of any return.
- **Manufactured dividends.** Stock Token dividends are paid by raising the token's multiplier. Lenders receive them
  automatically; borrowers owe them, because they must return the same number of wrapped units.
- **Issuer powers.** The Stock Token issuer can pause transfers, blocklist addresses (including Stockline's
  contracts) and burn balances. While a token is paused, exits that move that token wait for the issuer.
- **USDG.** The USDG issuer can freeze and wipe balances, including those held by Stockline contracts or Morpho.
- **Oracles and smart contracts.** Stockline relies on Chainlink feeds, Morpho Blue, Morpho Vault V2 and its own
  contracts. Bugs, oracle failures and chain outages can cause losses. Liquidations after an outage cannot be delayed.
- **Testnet.** On the testnet all assets are test assets with no value, and the service may be reset at any time.
